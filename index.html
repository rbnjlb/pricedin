What we do

Every headline has a price tag. Priced In finds out who pays, who profits, and what it means for your money. We cover the biggest stories in AI, tech, markets and the economy, with real numbers and real sources. No hype.

Follow the moneyEvery episode asks the same questions: who pays, who wins, and how it could end.

How the videos are made

Priced In is produced and supervised by its creator. The illustrations are AI-generated paintings. The narration uses an AI clone of the creator's own voice. Charts are drawn from public market data. Photos of real people are free-licence photos (Creative Commons or public domain), credited under each video.
